In case you weren't able to attend, here is a recap of the night's festivities, including the newly installed leaders and all the well-deserved award winners and graduates.

REALTOR of the YEAR

This year's Realtor of the Year award goes to John Smith for his unwavering commitment to his clients and the real estate industry. His professionalism and dedication to providing exceptional service to all of his clients has made him stand out as an exemplary real estate professional. He is a model of excellence and a true asset to the real estate industry. Congratulations John!

Local Real Estate

RPAC Broker Cup

The RPAC Broker Cup is a prestigious award that recognizes the office who is the most engaged with political issues in the real estate industry. The award is a testament to the hard work and dedication of Local Real Estate, Co. and serves to honor their commitment to professionalism and home ownership. We are proud to congratulate them for their exceptional efforts and for achieving this distinguished recognition.

Ursula Weinkauff

Global Diversity Achievement Award

Ursula Weinkauff is the proud recipient of the Global Diversity Achievement Award. This award recognizes her efforts in establishing a strong global presence in the real estate industry through her dedication and hard work. Her commitment to diversity and inclusion has helped her to create meaningful relationships both in the local and international communities. Ursula is a true trailblazer and leader in the real estate industry.

The Young Professionals Network 4 under 40 award is a prestigious honor given to the four up and coming real estate professionals who are expected to be the next face of local real estate. This year's recipients are Eddie Burnham, Jake Comisar, My Mai, and John Bolen. These four individuals have already achieved a great deal in their careers and are well-deserving of this recognition. Congratulations to the Young Professionals Network 4 under 40!

Andy Lynch

Pat Lord Community Service Award

We are proud to announce that Andy Lynch is the recipient of the Pat Lord Community Service Award. He has proven to be an incredible asset to the community during the aftermath of Hurricane Ian, selflessly donating his time, energy and resources to help the community rebuild and recover. He is an active member of the local community and is involved in a variety of charitable organizations. We are proud to recognize Andy's commitment to service and to the community, and congratulate him on this well-deserved award.

Jeanne Drath

Roger Brunswick Advocate Award

Jeanne Drath is the proud recipient of the Roger Brunswick Advocate Award for exemplifying the spirit of advocacy and being a leader in the real estate industry. She has been involved with the Bonita Estero Realtors for many years and is passionate about the cause. This award is dedicated to the late Roger Brunswick who was a leader in the movement to make the real estate industry more inclusive and accessible. Jeanne is an embodiment of Roger's spirit and is honored to receive this award. Congratulations to Jeanne for this well-deserved award.

Dena Wilcoxen

David Hengel Educator of the Year Award

This year, the David Hengel Educator of the Year Award goes to Dena Wilcoxen for her outstanding dedication to educating realtors over the past year. Dena has gone above and beyond to ensure that the realtors she works with are always learning and growing, and her presentation on “Professionalism” is a testament to her commitment to the education of professionals in her field. Dena is an inspiring example of a rising realtor educator and a pillar of our industry. We are proud to recognize Dena Wilcoxen, Past President, for her hard work and dedication, and congratulate her on this prestigious award.

Jonathan Van Heukelom

Breakthrough Agent of the Year

We are proud to present Jonathan Van Heukelom with the Breakthrough Agent of the Year award for his tremendous strides in the real estate industry over the past year. His client-first approach, dedication to hard work, and attention to detail, along with the incredible impact he and his wife Kristin have made in the lives of their clients, make him a true standout among his peers. We wish Jonathan and Kristin continued success in all their future endeavors. Congratulations Jonathan!
