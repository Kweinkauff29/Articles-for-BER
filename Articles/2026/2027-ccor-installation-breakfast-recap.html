2027 CCOR Installation Breakfast Recap

A morning to thank our 2026 leaders, welcome the 2027 Board of Directors and look ahead to the year in front of us.

Thursday, November 19, 2026 • Spanish Wells Golf & Country Club, Bonita Springs

Published November 24, 2026 • By Coconut Coast Organization of REALTORS®

Every installation is a handoff. At the 2027 CCOR Installation Breakfast, outgoing 2026 President Jeanne Drath passed leadership to 2027 President Wade Abed, and a new Board of Directors stepped forward to serve.

The breakfast was held on Thursday, November 19, 2026, at Spanish Wells Golf & Country Club in Bonita Springs. The program carried the theme Collaborate. Educate. Elevate. Nigel Fullick was listed as emcee and Angie Parker as Installing Officer. Jennifer Henry was the featured speaker, with a talk titled The Importance of Connection, and the BSHS Jazz Band was scheduled to provide the music.

To Jeanne and everyone who served in 2026, thank you. To Wade and the incoming leadership team, congratulations. Below you will find photos from the morning, the people leading CCOR in 2027, a few things we accomplished in 2026, and the full program.

A Few 2026 Accomplishments

Before turning the page, here are a few of the 2026 accomplishments recognized in the program.

  • A new name for our 60th year (January–August 2026): Members voted in January to adopt the Coconut Coast name. After NAR approved the change, CCOR celebrated its new identity with members and partners at the August 13 kickoff.
  • Helping homeowners spot property fraud (2026 homeowner outreach): CCOR partnered with the Lee County Clerk of Courts to promote free property fraud alerts through the Protect Our Homes campaign.
  • MLS collaboration tools reached members early (January–June 2026): Bonita Springs–Estero was among the first MLS markets in the Realtor.com+ rollout, and in June CCOR introduced OneHomeowner through Matrix.
  • Southwest Florida on the global stage (August 27–28, 2026): CCOR President Jeanne Drath and Treasurer Gina Lamouth represented Southwest Florida at CIMI360 in Rio de Janeiro, connecting with real estate professionals and potential referral partners.
