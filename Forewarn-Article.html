Forewarn - Your Newest Lifesaving Member Benefit


August 2nd, 2022

Do you know who you are working with?

Being a real estate agent is inherently a very dangerous job. We meet random people at vacant properties and put ourselves in very vulnerable situations for the sake of business and to get people into the homes of their dreams. It’s not fair to assume that everyone is malicious or means you harm but if you roll the dice enough, you may find yourself meeting someone with bad intentions. That is why, in an effort to mitigate the danger you might face, Bonita Springs-Estero Realtors purchased access to FOREWARN for each of our members and if you are reading this, it’s now available!

What is Forewarn?

Forewarn is an easy/quick-to-use tool to help you understand who you are working with before you meet them. Within a few seconds, you will be able to search via phone number or contact details and confirm their identity and check their criminal and financial records. With Forewarn, you will be instantly able to verify:
  • Identity (first name, last name, alias, and age)
  • Phone numbers (residential and mobile history)
  • Address history (rental, ownership, and associated addresses)
  • Financial indicators (bankruptcy, lien, and judgement history)
  • Property records (nationwide property ownership history)
  • Criminal records (nationwide criminal history search)

Getting Started with FOREWARN

Forewarn is now available for all Bonita Springs-Estero Realtors Primary and Secondary members. Signing up is easy. Click on the "Claim my account" button below, enter your email and set your password. Within seconds, you will be prompted to log into the service and you are on your way to verifying your clients.
