As the rental market in Southwest Florida continues accelerating, members of Bonita Springs-Estero Realtors can take advantage of a comprehensive suite to manage rentals efficiently. RentSpree, a free tool for BER members, lets you screen tenants instantly in just a few clicks and manages your entire rental process seamlessly.

When creating a lease listing within Matrix, you can generate a link to a secure online application that can be completed in minutes. Renters can access the application in different ways: directly via an ApplyLink™, or through an email or text message invitation. This integration saves time and costs, as there are no fees for using RentSpree – and the process can be kicked off without ever needing to leave Matrix.

The screening package provides crucial information on applicants, including a rental application, credit report, background check and eviction-related proceedings. Renters pay only a $38 fee to apply to your listing; once they initiate the screening process, you can track their progress every step of the way. Even better, credit reports through RentSpree only require a soft pull, with no damage to applicants’ scores. Applicants can use their reports to apply for free to other listings if they are denied to their first choice property.

How to Screen Tenants With RentSpree:



You can also purchase a Rent Estimate Report from RentSpree to assist in determining the optimum listing price for a rental. To get an even more detailed picture of an applicant, RentSpree PRO is also available to check references and income verification documents in one step. PRO is available for $9.99 a month, but you can experience a free month trial to familiarize yourself with its features.

Looking to market your rentals? RentSpree offers agent profiles and listing pages so you can advertise your personal brand and your rentals in one place. You can also leverage our rental client manager to track prospective renters and help maintain a relationship with them.

This rental tool is the most reviewed rental screening software on Google with a stellar 4.7 out of 5 rating based on 1,000+ reviews. BER and RentSpree’s goal is to provide a user-friendly experience to agents, renters, property managers and landlords.

Opt in for screening the next time you add a rental into Matrix, or you can always learn more at http://rentspree.com/ber.
