Realtor.com+ Launches — Bonita Springs-Estero MLS Among the First Markets

Published January 21, 2026 • Partnership Announcement

Big news for BER members: Bonita Springs-Estero REALTORS® is proud to be among the first MLS markets included in the launch of Realtor.com+. This new platform brings agents and clients into a branded, collaborative space where search, listings, and messages are all under one roof.

What Is Realtor.com+?

Realtor.com+ is a new collaboration platform that connects agents and consumers in a unified experience. It's designed to keep everyone in sync from the first click to the closing table. The platform combines the power of Realtor.com's industry-leading property search with tools that foster real-time communication between agents and their clients.

Keep transactions moving forward with streamlined communication from click to close.

Why This Matters for BER Members

Being among the first markets included in the Realtor.com+ launch means our members will be at the forefront of this new technology. Here's what this partnership signals:

✨ Key Benefits

  • Early Access: BER members will be among the first agents nationally to leverage Realtor.com+ with their clients.
  • Branded Experience: Work within a professional, agent-branded platform that keeps you connected to your clients throughout their home search.
  • Streamlined Workflows: Consolidate search, listings, and messaging into a single collaborative environment.
  • Industry Recognition: Our participation reflects the strength and reputation of the Bonita Springs-Estero MLS.

✅ No Changes to Your Membership

This is a new benefit we're excited to bring to our members. There are no plans to remove any current membership benefits or raise prices. Realtor.com+ is simply another service we want to provide so you can get ahead in your business!
