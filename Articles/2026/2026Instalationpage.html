2026 Installation & Awards Banquet Recap

“Give Back, Grow Forward” — Celebrating 60 Years of BER Leadership

Bonita Springs-Estero REALTORS® • 2026 Installation & Awards Banquet Recap

Bonita Springs-Estero REALTORS® came together to celebrate our 2026 Installation & Awards Banquet and the theme of the year: Give Back, Grow Forward. It was a night to honor our 2025 “Of the Year” award recipients, welcome our 2026 leadership team, and thank the partners and sponsors who make our work in the community possible.

We also extend a heartfelt congratulations to Jeanne Drath on being installed as the 2026 President of Bonita Springs-Estero REALTORS®. Her message to members centered on service, community, and continued growth as we move into the next 60 years of BER.
