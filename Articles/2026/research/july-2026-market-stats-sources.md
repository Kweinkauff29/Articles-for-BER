## Core thesis

The July data supports a two-part conclusion:

1. Market-clearing conditions improved across all four local markets. Homes for sale and months of supply declined year over year, and closed sales represented a larger share of available homes.
2. Price response remained micro-market-specific. Estero and Naples increased while Bonita Springs and Fort Myers declined.

Therefore, July was **more efficient and less overstocked**, but it was not one uniform price rebound.

## Important source inconsistencies

- Both supplied report decks use a cover page reading **June 2026 Market Report**, but the individual data charts consistently say **July 2026**. The article follows the chart month, not the cover.
- The Estero price slide says **Med. Sales Price** in the callout but **Average Sales Price** under the chart. The article uses the neutral phrase **reported price benchmark** and flags the issue for verification before publication.
- The closed-sales-to-homes-for-sale ratio is an original descriptive calculation for this article. It is not an official absorption-rate or months-of-supply statistic.
